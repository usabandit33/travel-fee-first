Questions people ask after a mobile notary quote jumps

Is the Oregon stamp fee the same as the mobile travel fee?

No. The act fee and the drive fee are two numbers. Oregon expects the travel fee to be agreed before the notary starts traveling. Ask both.

Why is an evening hospital visit more than a daytime house call?

After-hours, holiday, weather, and facility checkpoints often add a published travel bump. Ask which clock and which address.

What ID do I need?

Current government photo ID that matches the name on the paper. Ask the shop if your ID type is accepted before they drive.

Can I sign before the notary arrives?

No. Sign in front of the notary. Keep the papers unsigned.

Is remote online notarization the same as a mobile visit?

No. Remote is a video session. Some papers still need an in-person stamp. Ask which path your paper needs.

Do I need extra witnesses?

Some papers do. Ask if the shop can bring a witness and the fee. Portland phone numbers are listed so you can ask more than one shop the same questions.
