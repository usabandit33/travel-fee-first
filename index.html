Need a notary at the house. Ask the travel fee before anyone starts the car.

Portland families mix the state stamp fee with the drive fee. After-hours hospital visits and extra wait minutes are where the bill jumps. Use this list before you book.

  • Ask the travel fee and the per-stamp fee as two numbers. Agree before they drive.
  • Ask if this slot is daytime or after-hours / weekend / holiday.
  • Ask how many on-site minutes are included and the fee if a second signer is late.
  • Have current photo ID. Do not sign until the notary is there.
  • Ask whether the paper can be done in person only or by remote online notarization.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
